Projects

Technical innovations and financial software built by FTC

FTC Screener

A full-stack research platform that replaces the five tabs Indian market analysis usually takes. Screen the index on sector, P/E, P/B, dividend yield, beta and 52-week position, compare three companies side by side, and export to CSV. At its centre sits a transparent 0-100 Market Mood Index built from six weighted inputs.

Research & Readings

Reports published by the council's Private and Public Markets verticals

Private Markets 1 Report

Protein: India's High-Protein Foods & Snacks Market

The GLP-1 Inflection Point

By FTC Private Markets Team

A sectoral study of India's high-protein food and snacks category, built on a 126-respondent consumer survey. It maps the country's standing protein deficit against the demand shift created by the semaglutide patent cliff, then works through the competitive landscape, market sizing, regulatory headwinds and the white spaces left open to new entrants.

Public Markets 2 Reports

An Introduction to the Nifty 50 Index

India's Benchmark Equity Index: Structure, History and Significance

By FTC Public Markets Team

A ground-up guide to India's flagship equity index, starting from what a share actually is. It covers the index's origins and governance, the float-adjusted calculation method, how the fifty constituents are selected and rebalanced, the sectoral mix, and the routes available to investors who want to track it.

A Decade of UPI

India's Digital Payment Revolution: Finance, Banking and Economic Impact

By FTC Public Markets Team

Ten years of the Unified Payments Interface, from its April 2016 pilot to over 24,000 crore transactions worth ₹314 lakh crore in FY 2024-25. The report traces the milestones that drove adoption, the platform's economic and banking impact, and the cybersecurity, monetisation and scalability questions facing its second decade.
